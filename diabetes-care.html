What the user experiences

Screening guidance that feels like a conversation.

Instead of presenting one long form, the assistant asks a relevant question, accepts simple answers and adjusts the next step using the information already provided.

💬

Easy guided interaction

Users answer through simple choices, numbers or “Not sure,” helping them complete the assessment without needing medical or technical knowledge.

🧭

Adaptive question flow

Questions depend on previous answers—for example, pregnancy-related history is shown only when relevant.

📊

Clear risk indication

The result presents a lower, moderate or higher likelihood, explains contributing factors and suggests sensible next actions.

Assessment coverage

A structured view of the factors that matter.

The assistant gathers multiple signals before presenting guidance, while allowing unknown values to be safely skipped.

01

Core health details

Age, gender, height, weight, fasting blood sugar, HbA1c and blood group.

02

Health and family history

Immediate family history, gestational diabetes where relevant, thyroid conditions and high blood pressure.

03

Common symptoms

Frequent thirst or urination, fatigue, blurred vision, slow wound healing, tingling, infections and unexplained pain.

04

Lipid and body indicators

Waist circumference, total cholesterol, HDL and triglycerides when the user knows them.

05

Lifestyle context

Physical activity, smoking and typical diet patterns are included in the conversation.

06

Additional report notes

Users can add recent laboratory values or skip the section when no report is available.

Responsible by design

Guidance, not a diagnosis.

The assistant clearly states that its result is a general advisory indication based on user-provided information. It directs users to qualified medical professionals and proper laboratory testing for an accurate clinical assessment.

  • Does not claim to diagnose diabetes
  • Unknown values do not break the conversation
  • Suggestions change with the indicated risk level
  • Users can restart the guided assessment
  • Suitable as a demonstrator for clinics, wellness programmes and health education

Important: This product provides general awareness and screening support only. It is not a medical device and must not replace diagnosis, emergency care or advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
