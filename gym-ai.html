Built around gym-owner problems

Every feature has a clear business job.

Less manual enquiry work, faster response and a better path from website visit to membership.

01

Convert more visitors into trials

The problem: A static page loses visitors who are unsure what to do next.

The outcome: Quick choices for Plans, Timings, Personal Training, Free Trial, Facilities, Location and Reviews guide the conversation. A three-step Find My Plan journey recommends a suitable option, while the one-day free-pass form captures the lead immediately.

02

Handle enquiries 24/7 without adding staff

The problem: Pricing, trainer and timing questions arrive when the front desk is busy or closed.

The outcome: The assistant answers from the gyms approved information on both the website and WhatsApp, helping prospects continue instead of leaving unanswered.

03

Update answers from one source

The problem: Owners should not maintain long FAQ pages or repeatedly train staff on every answer.

The outcome: Upload one gym information document to create the assistants question-and-answer knowledge. Update the document when plans, schedules or policies change.

04

Meet prospects on WhatsApp and web

The problem: Many Indian customers prefer WhatsApp to web forms or calls.

The outcome: The same approved gym information supports both channels, keeping answers consistent and giving visitors a familiar way to continue.

05

Turn captured leads into a visible pipeline

The problem: A lead has little value when it sits in an unchecked spreadsheet.

The outcome: The owner dashboard tracks each stage and lets the team open a WhatsApp follow-up from the same screen, closing the gap between enquiry and sales action.

06

Build trust before the gym visit

The problem: Prospects check location, reviews and recent activity before committing.

The outcome: Google Reviews, Google Maps and Instagram can be presented with the gym website so customers can validate the business and plan their visit.

07

Control AI cost and protect personal details

The problem: Sending every message to an AI model increases cost, while names and phone numbers should not be exposed unnecessarily.

The outcome: Lead details are handled separately from question answering. Routine capture and phone detection avoid unnecessary AI calls, while the reasoning model is used only when a real gym question needs an answer.
